Effective date: 21 November 2026

TradingLve ("we", "our", or "us") provides the TradingLve Gold Calculator Android application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

The app is designed to provide gold calculations and USD-to-INR currency conversion. Based on the app's current functionality, we do not ask you to create an account and do not collect or store personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, precise location, or contacts.

We do not use the app to serve advertisements or to track users across apps or websites.

2. Calculations and Information Stored on Your Device

Values you enter for calculations are used to provide the requested results. We do not intentionally transmit or store those entered values on our own servers.

3. Currency Rates and Third-Party Services

If a version of the app retrieves currency-rate information from an external provider, the request may be processed by that provider. That provider may receive technical information such as an IP address as part of normal internet communication. The provider's own privacy policy will apply to its processing. We do not control third-party services.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information because the app is not designed to collect personal information. This does not cover technical information independently processed by an external currency-rate provider, if one is used.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. Since the app does not require an account and is not intended to collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the app's functionality simple and limit the information it handles. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
